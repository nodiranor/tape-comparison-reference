Automotive Wire Harness Tape Types Compared

An engineering reference comparing the six tape constructions most commonly used to wrap automotive wire harnesses — fleece, velvet (flock), PVC, foam, felt, and woven cloth — by material, typical application, temperature range, abrasion resistance, and noise-damping performance.

汽车线束胶带对比

本页对比汽车线束包扎中最常用的六种胶带——植绒胶带、无纺布胶带、PVC胶带、泡棉胶带、毛毡胶带、布基胶带——从材质、典型应用部位、耐温范围、耐磨性和降噪性能五个维度提供工程选型参考。

Velvet (Flock) Tape

Material: PET flock fibers bonded to a woven or PVC backing, soft plush texture.

Typical use: Cabin-facing harness runs near trim and panels — squeak and rattle prevention.

植绒胶带（絨面胶带）

材质：PET植绒纤维粘合在无纺布或PVC基材上，表面柔软细腻。

典型用途：贴近内饰面板的线束段，防止异响和抖动噪音。

Fleece Tape

Material: Non-woven polyester fleece, soft matte surface, breathable.

Typical use: General-purpose interior bundling, standard cost-effective wrap.

无纺布胶带（绒布胶带）

材质：无纺聚酯纤维布，表面柔软哑光，透气性好。

典型用途：车内一般线束包扎，性价比高的标准包扎材料。

Temp -40 to 125°C Abrasion ●●●○○ Noise damping ●●●●○ 耐温 -40 至 125°C 耐磨性 ●●●○○ 降噪性 ●●●●○

PVC Tape

Material: Plasticized PVC film, glossy or matte, flame-retardant grades common.

Typical use: Branch-off points, connector strain relief, general-purpose wrap.

PVC胶带

材质：增塑聚氯乙烯薄膜，光面或哑光面，常见阻燃等级。

典型用途：线束分支点包扎、接插件应力消除、通用型包扎。

Temp -18 to 105°C Abrasion ●●●●○ Noise damping ●○○○○ 耐温 -18 至 105°C 耐磨性 ●●●●○ 降噪性 ●○○○○

Foam Tape

Material: Closed-cell PE/PU foam, cushioned, single or double-sided adhesive.

Typical use: Vibration damping, gap filling at pass-throughs and grommets.

泡棉胶带

材质：闭孔PE/PU泡棉，具缓冲性，单面或双面胶。

典型用途：过孔/护套处的减振和填缝密封。

Temp -40 to 90°C Abrasion ●●○○○ Noise damping ●●●●● 耐温 -40 至 90°C 耐磨性 ●●○○○ 降噪性 ●●●●●

Woven Cloth Tape

Material: Woven fabric coated with rubber or acrylic adhesive, high tensile strength.

Typical use: High-abrasion engine-bay and chassis routing, heat and chemical exposure.

布基胶带（棉纱胶带）

材质：编织布基涂覆橡胶或丙烯酸胶粘剂，抗拉强度高。

典型用途：发动机舱和底盘高磨损线束走线，耐热耐化学品。

Temp -40 to 130°C Abrasion ●●●●● Noise damping ●●○○○ 耐温 -40 至 130°C 耐磨性 ●●●●● 降噪性 ●●○○○

Felt Tape

Material: Dense felted fiber mat, thicker than fleece, heavy cushioning.

Typical use: Heavy noise and vibration damping on thick harness bundles.

毛毡胶带

材质：致密毡化纤维毡，比无纺布更厚，缓冲性强。

典型用途：较粗线束的重度降噪减振包扎。
